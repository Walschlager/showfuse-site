ShowFuse is a swipe-first app for finding live music near you. The artists worth hearing, the dates they're playing, and the rooms they play them in. Follow an artist and their next show near you finds its way back to your deck.

iOS · Closed beta

How it works

01
Swipe the deck.

Artists near you, with their music playing as you browse. Right to follow, up when you're going.

02
Never miss the date.

Artists you follow come back as a show card when they're playing close, with the ticket link at the seller's own door.

03
Built for artists too.

Post your dates, see who's going and who said maybe, city by city.
