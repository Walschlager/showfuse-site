FAQ

Closed beta · iOS

What is ShowFuse?

A swipe-first app for discovering live music near you. Artists, their upcoming shows, and the venues they play. Follow an artist and their next show near you comes back to your deck.

Does ShowFuse sell tickets?

No. We link you to the seller's own page (Ticketmaster, AXS, the venue's box office, and others). The purchase, price, fees, and refunds are with that seller. See our Terms.

How do I get in?

The beta is invite-only right now. If a friend sent you a link, ask them for an invite. Public availability will be announced here and in the app.

Do I have to share my location?

No. You can pick a city manually. If you allow location, we use it at city level to find shows near you; precise location is only used if you explicitly allow it.

I'm an artist. What can I do?

Claim or create your artist profile, post your dates, add a 30-second clip of your music, and see who's going and who said maybe, city by city.

How do direct messages work?

Messages are off by default and require a request the other person accepts. Images can't be sent in messages, by design. Long-press any message or profile to report it; a real person reviews every report.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account. It removes your account and data immediately.

Something's broken.

It's a beta, so thank you for telling us. Email info@showfuseapp.com with what you tapped and what happened.
