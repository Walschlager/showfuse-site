PRIVACY POLICY

Effective November 25, 2026 · Beta

We don't sell your personal data. This page says what ShowFuse collects, why, who touches it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account info you give us: email, display name, username, role (listener, artist, or venue), and date of birth (used only to confirm age).
  • Content you post: profile fields, photos, artist clips, show chats, and direct messages.
  • Activity in the app: swipes, follows, shows you mark as going or maybe, reminders, and reports. This is what makes Discover work.
  • Location: city-level by default. Precise location only if you explicitly allow it, and only to find shows near you. You can pick a city manually instead.
  • Device info: OS version, app build, and crash logs.

How we use it

To personalize Discover, surface nearby shows, deliver messages, prevent abuse, send essential service notifications, fix bugs, and build anonymized, aggregated trends about live-music demand. Aggregated data never identifies you.

What we never do

  • Sell personal data
  • Share precise location with advertisers
  • Read your direct messages to train models
  • Buy data from third parties to enrich your profile

Who else touches your data

We use a small number of service providers to run ShowFuse. They handle data only to provide their service to us, under their own policies:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers, which run our servers, database and media storage.
  • Email delivery providers, which send our emails, like sign-in codes.
  • App stores and device platforms, for the app itself, push notifications and, if offered, subscriptions.
  • Map providers, for maps, places and address search.
  • Network and security providers, which help keep the site fast and protected.
  • An AI service provider, which helps our team review artist claims (see "Artist claims" below).
  • Payment processors, only if you buy merch. They take the payment, and we never see your card number.

You also reach other companies through ShowFuse:

  • Music services that supply the song previews and some artist pictures. When you play a preview, the audio comes from them.
  • Ticket sellers. Tapping a ticket link takes you to the seller; from there their privacy policy applies, not ours.
  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them (see the next section).

Signing in with Google or Apple

You can sign in to ShowFuse with your Google or Apple account instead of a password. It's optional.

  • What we get: your name, your email address, and an account ID from Google or Apple. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we get a private relay address instead of your real one, and Apple forwards our emails to you. Google also includes a link to your profile picture; we don't use or keep it.
  • What we use it for: only to create your ShowFuse account, sign you in, and keep your account secure, for example to make sure one Google or Apple login can't be attached to two accounts.
  • What we don't get or do: we never see your Google or Apple password, we don't ask for your contacts, calendar, photos or anything else in those accounts, and we never post anything for you.
  • Disconnecting: you can remove Google or Apple any time in Settings → Account Security. Deleting your ShowFuse account removes them too, and we ask Apple to revoke our access.

ShowFuse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Artist claims

When you claim an artist page at showfuseapp.com/claim, here's what we collect and why:

  • Your email, to send you a code and our answer.
  • Your birthday, only if we don't have it yet, to check you're 18 or older.
  • Links you add (like your socials or website) and your manager's or label's email, if you give them. Your links go on your page once it's approved.
  • Proof screenshots. We keep them private, use them only to review your claim, and delete them 30 days after we decide on it.
  • Messages you send us. If you send us your claim code in a direct message on a social network, we see the account that sent it and the message.

To confirm it's really you, we may check public information, like the artist's official website and social accounts. Automated tools, including an AI service provider, help our team review claims and reports. They get the claim's details, your links and your screenshots, but never your full email address or your birthday. They never decide anything: a person on our team makes every decision, and an invite we send you counts as that approval. We keep a record of each claim and decision so we can protect the page later.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data. Delete Account in Settings closes your account right away and permanently deletes it and its data after 7 days. During those 7 days you can sign in and keep your account. Written requests to info@showfuseapp.com get a response within 30 days. Residents of California, the EU and UK, and other applicable jurisdictions have additional statutory rights, which we honor.

Retention

Account data stays until you delete it. Crash logs are kept 90 days. Anonymized aggregate data may be kept indefinitely.

Children

New accounts and artist claims must be 18 or older, and every account confirms a date of birth at signup. ShowFuse is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

We'll announce material changes in the app or by email. The effective date at the top tells you when this page last changed.
