PRIVACY POLICY

Effective September 2, 2026 · Beta

We don't sell your personal data. This page says what ShowFuse collects, why, who touches it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account info you give us: email, display name, username, role (listener, artist, or venue), and date of birth (used only to confirm age).
  • Content you post: profile fields, photos, artist clips, show chats, and direct messages.
  • Activity in the app: swipes, follows, shows you mark as going or maybe, reminders, and reports. This is what makes Discover work.
  • Location: city-level by default. Precise location only if you explicitly allow it, and only to find shows near you. You can pick a city manually instead.
  • Device info: OS version, app build, and crash logs.

How we use it

To personalize Discover, surface nearby shows, deliver messages, prevent abuse, send essential service notifications, fix bugs, and build anonymized, aggregated trends about live-music demand. Aggregated data never identifies you.

What we never do

  • Sell personal data
  • Share precise location with advertisers
  • Read your direct messages to train models
  • Buy data from third parties to enrich your profile

Who else touches your data

Apple (device services and, if offered, subscriptions), Railway (API hosting, database, and media storage), Deezer (artist previews and catalog data), and ticket sellers such as Ticketmaster, AXS, Eventbrite, and DICE (show discovery and ticket links). Tapping a ticket link takes you to that seller; from there their privacy policy applies, not ours. Each provider handles data under its own policy.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data. Delete Account in Settings removes your account and its data immediately. Written requests to info@showfuseapp.com get a response within 30 days. Residents of California, the EU and UK, and other applicable jurisdictions have additional statutory rights, which we honor.

Retention

Account data stays until you delete it. Crash logs are kept 90 days. Anonymized aggregate data may be kept indefinitely.

Children

ShowFuse is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Accounts must confirm a date of birth at signup.

Changes

We'll announce material changes in the app or by email. The effective date at the top tells you when this page last changed.
