SHIPPING & RETURNS

Returns and refunds · Effective from the day the shop opens

You can return most merch within 30 days of the day we ship it. Ask us first, send it back, and we refund you after we receive it and check it.

The short version

  • You have 30 days from the day we ship your order to ask for a return.
  • Ask first, in the app or on your order page. Please don't send anything before we say yes. A package sent without a request may be refused.
  • You pay to send it back, unless it's our mistake: it arrived damaged or defective, or we sent the wrong item. Then we cover it.
  • We refund only after we receive your package and check what's inside. The refund goes back to the payment method you used.
  • Items marked Final sale can't be returned.

What you can return

  • Most items, within 30 days of the day we ship your order. That date is in your shipping email and on your order page. It is not the day the parcel arrives.
  • Unworn, unwashed, with the tags on, and in the original packaging where possible.
  • Some of an order, or all of it. Pick just the items you want to send back. That is a partial return (see below).
  • Not Final sale items. “Final sale” means we marked the item that way before you bought it, for example a limited run or a signed item. You'll see the tag on the product page, in your bag and on your order. Final-sale items can't be returned. If one arrives damaged, or it isn't the item you ordered, we'll still fix it (see “Damaged, defective or wrong item”).

How to return something

  1. Ask first. Open your order (in the app, My orders; on the website, the “View your order” link in your email) and press Return or refund. Pick the items, tell us why and, if it arrived damaged or wrong, add a photo.
  2. We reply by email within 1 to 2 business days. If we approve it, the email has the return address and instructions. If we can't, it says why.
  3. Send it within 14 days of the day we approve it. Write your order number on the box, use a service with tracking, and keep your receipt. You can add your tracking number to your order page (it is optional).
  4. We check it, then refund you. See “When and how we refund”.
No return without approval. If you send something without a request, we may refuse the package.

Who pays for return shipping

  • You do, for any reason except our mistake.
  • We do when it's our mistake: the item arrived damaged or defective, or we sent the wrong item. Pay for the postage, keep the receipt, and add what you paid on your order page. We add it to your refund. (Prepaid return labels are coming later.)
  • Canada. The same rules apply. You pay the return postage (we pay it when it's our mistake) and any customs, duties or fees charged on the way back. We can't refund those.

When and how we refund

  • Only after we receive your package and check what's inside. Not when you send it, and not when we approve it.
  • The refund goes back to the payment method you used, through Stripe. We email you when we've made it. Banks usually take about 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • We refund the price of the items you send back, and the sales tax we charged on them.
  • We don't refund the original shipping, unless it was our mistake. Then we refund the shipping you paid on that order too.
  • Canada: we can't refund import taxes, duties or carrier fees you paid.

Partial returns

You can send back some items and keep the rest. We refund the items you send back and the tax on them. Shipping isn't refunded on a partial return, unless it was our mistake.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

  • Tell us as soon as you see it. Press Return or refund and pick Damaged or defective or Wrong item sent. A photo helps. This works for Final sale items too. If your 30 days have passed, write to us anyway and we'll look at it.
  • We make it right. We cover the return shipping, and we refund the item and the shipping you paid. If you'd rather have a replacement and we have it, say so when you write to us.
  • We still check the item when it arrives, and we refund after that, like any other return.

If what we receive isn't right

If the package arrives with items that are worn, washed, damaged by use, missing tags or parts, or different from what we approved, we may refund only part of the price, or nothing. We'll email you what we found and why. If you think we got it wrong, write to us.

Lost return packages

If a return gets lost on its way back to us, we can't refund what doesn't arrive. So please use a service with tracking and keep your receipt. If tracking says it was delivered to us and we can't find it, write to us and we'll look into it with you.

Exchanges and return labels

Size exchanges and prepaid return labels are coming later. Until then, if it doesn't fit, return it for a refund and order the size you need.

Cancelling before it ships

This page is about returns after your order ships. To cancel an order that hasn't shipped, write to us right away. If we haven't shipped it, we'll cancel it and refund you in full.

Chargebacks

Please contact us first. We fix real problems quickly. If you ask your bank or card company to reverse a payment (a chargeback), we'll give them the records of your order: what you bought, the tracking, our emails and your return request. Please don't dispute an order we've already refunded, or a return that is still open.

How to contact us

Email info@showfuseapp.com, or use the form at showfuseapp.com/support and pick Merch or an order. Please include your order number (like SF-1002) and, for a return, your request number (like RET-0007). We answer by email, usually within one business day. Return requests: within 1 to 2 business days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The version in effect when you place an order applies to that order.
